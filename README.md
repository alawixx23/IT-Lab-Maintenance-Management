IT-Lab-Maintenance-Management
___________________________________
Project Purpose
1. To develop a digital system that helps manage and organize computer laboratory maintenance records, making it easier to monitor equipment conditions and maintenance activities.

2. To provide an efficient way of recording technical problems, tracking repair activities, and monitoring the maintenance history of computers and other laboratory equipment.

3. To improve the management of computer laboratory resources by helping IT personnel identify issues, schedule maintenance, and maintain equipment in good working condition.

__________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________   
Planned Features
1. Maintenance Log Management – Allows users to add, view, update, and delete maintenance records, including repair details, dates, and reported issues.

2. Computer Equipment Monitoring – Provides a list of laboratory computers and equipment with their current conditions and status (Working, Under Maintenance, or Defective).

3. Issue and Repair Tracking – Allows users to report technical problems, record troubleshooting activities, and monitor repair progress.

4. Maintenance History – Stores previous maintenance activities for each computer or equipment for easy reference.

5. Dashboard and Reports – Displays the total number of computers, working equipment, defective units, and ongoing maintenance activities.

6. User Login and Access Management – Provides secure access for administrators and authorized IT personnel.
